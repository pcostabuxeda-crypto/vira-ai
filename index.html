AI video studio

Short educational videos, explained in one minute.

Vira AI turns real news articles and verified facts into short vertical videos presented by the same animated host, Lucía, with data charts and kinetic typography. Every video is reviewed by the creator before it is published to their own channels.

Grounded in real sources

Scripts are written only from the text of a published article or well-established facts, and the source is credited in the video description.

One consistent presenter

An animated presenter, charts and on-screen text are rendered by code, so every video has the same look and voice.

Human review first

Nothing is posted automatically to TikTok. The creator previews each video, edits the caption and chooses the settings before sending it.

How it works

  1. Pick a topic. Vira AI reads public news feeds and selects a topic that has not been covered yet.
  2. Write the script. An AI model writes a short script using only facts from the source article.
  3. Animate. The presenter, charts and captions are rendered into a 9:16 video with a synthetic voice.
  4. Review. The creator watches the video and edits the title, caption and hashtags in the Vira AI dashboard.
  5. Publish. After explicit confirmation, the video is sent to the creator's own connected YouTube or TikTok account.

How Vira AI uses TikTok

Login Kit

The creator signs in with TikTok to connect their own account. Vira AI reads only the basic profile (display name, avatar and open ID) to show which account will receive the video.

Content Posting API

The finished video is uploaded to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft, or posted with the privacy level, interaction settings and commercial-content disclosure the creator selects in the review screen.

Clear labelling

Videos are marked as AI-generated content, and the review screen shows TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and, when relevant, the Branded Content Policy before posting.

Vira AI never posts without the creator's explicit action, never selects a privacy level by default, and lets the creator disconnect their TikTok account at any time.
