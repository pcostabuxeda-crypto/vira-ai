Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what information Vira AI processes when a creator connects a YouTube, TikTok or Instagram account, and how that information is used, stored and deleted.

1. Who we are

Vira AI is an AI-assisted studio for creating short educational videos and publishing them to the creator's own social media accounts. It runs on a private server operated by the Vira AI administrator.

2. Information we process

  • Content: topics, scripts, captions, images, synthetic voice audio and videos generated in Vira AI.
  • TikTok account information: the open ID, display name, username and avatar returned by TikTok Login Kit, and the creator settings returned by the Content Posting API (available privacy levels, interaction settings and maximum video length).
  • YouTube and Instagram account information: the channel or account ID, name and handle returned after authorisation.
  • Access tokens: OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to publish on the creator's behalf.
  • Technical data: limited server logs used for security and troubleshooting.

3. How we use it

  • To show the creator which account a video will be published to.
  • To upload or publish a video only after the creator reviews it and confirms the action.
  • To measure the public view counts of the creator's own published videos, so the creator can see which formats work best.

We do not sell, rent or share account information or tokens with third parties, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use TikTok or Google user data to train AI models.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are stored on the Vira AI server. The server is only reachable through a private network, except for temporary, randomly named links used to deliver video files to Instagram, which expire after one hour.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until the creator disconnects the account in Vira AI (Channels → Disconnect), which deletes them immediately.
  • The creator can also revoke access at any time from the platform: in TikTok, Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions; in Google, myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Generated videos and scripts remain on the server until the administrator deletes them.

6. Third-party services

Vira AI uses TikTok, Google/YouTube and Meta/Instagram APIs to publish content, and AI providers (Anthropic and OpenAI) and Microsoft speech services to generate scripts and voices. Only the content needed for each task (for example, the text of a script) is sent to those providers, never account tokens. Vira AI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and YouTube uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

7. Children

Vira AI is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. For questions or deletion requests, open an issue at github.com/pcostabuxeda-crypto/vira-ai/issues.
